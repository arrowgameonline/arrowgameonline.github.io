About Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle is the straightforward version of the grid-filling game. Pieces appear in a tray, you place them on the board, and completed rows and columns clear away.

There are no modes to pick and no tools to manage — only placement. Because nothing is hidden and nothing is timed, your score is a fairly honest measure of how well you read space. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Review the pieces in the tray before placing the first one.

  • Drag a piece onto the grid where it fits without fragmenting your open space.
  • Complete a full row or column to clear it.
  • Work from the edges inward so the middle of the board stays flexible.

Quick Tips

  • Keep remaining gaps the same shape as the pieces you keep being dealt.
  • The rule is one sentence. Staying alive past a few hundred points is the skill.
  • Nothing is hidden and nothing is timed.
