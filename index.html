Puzzle Game Tips

Feeling stuck is part of the loop, but a few habits make most of these boards easier to read.

  • Survey first. The free move is usually visible before you touch anything.
  • Work from the edge. Exposed pieces open space for the packed middle.
  • Rescan after every clear. One exit often unlocks three more.
  • Do not rush a second move. In traffic games, wait until the first car finishes.
  • Use fullscreen. A larger view makes dense grids and junctions easier to read.
  • Switch categories. A calm arrow board and a busy escape room train different eyes.
