About Bus Parking Out

Bus Parking Out runs two puzzles at the same time. Down below, arrow-marked buses are jammed into lanes and only move when the route ahead is clear.

Up top, passengers are queuing in colours — and a bus only leaves the bay once the matching passengers have boarded. Filling your last usable bay with the wrong colour can soft-lock the board. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Follow a bus's arrow toward the bays and check whether another bus occupies that lane.

  • If the move would collide, find the first bus in the lane and move that one instead.
  • Before committing, compare the bus colour against the passengers waiting above the bays.
  • Leave at least one bay workable — do not park a colour that nothing can board.

Quick Tips

  • When a bus bounces back, a specific vehicle is sitting in its arrow lane.
  • Keep one bay flexible so the terminal never runs out of useful moves.
  • A rearrangement tool recolours buses while leaving their arrows alone.
  • Rescan the lanes, bays and queue after every successful move.
