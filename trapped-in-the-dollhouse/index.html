About Trapped In The Dollhouse

Trapped In The Dollhouse is a point-and-click escape room with a deliberately unsettling atmosphere. You search a pink bedroom, a living room and the doors and toys between them.

There is no grid and no single rule to apply. Progress comes from noticing a detail and remembering it two rooms later. Rooms link together, so a discovery in one place unlocks another. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Inspect the current room and tap furniture, dolls, doors, screens and small objects.

  • Open close-up views on anything that might hide a clue.
  • Note unusual details — silhouettes, static, locks, symbols, object positions.
  • Carry discoveries between rooms, since one scene usually explains another.
