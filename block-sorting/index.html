About Block Sorting

Block Sorting is a colour-grouping puzzle on a grid of coloured blocks. You tap to work the board into clean colour sets.

The other block games here are about where a piece fits. This one is about which group to touch, and in what order. Moving one group inevitably disturbs its neighbours. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Scan the grid and identify which colours already sit close together.

  • Pick the group that needs the fewest moves to finish and start with it.
  • Check what a move will disturb before you make it, not after.
  • Leave the most scattered colour for last, when it has the most free space.

Quick Tips

  • Commit to one colour and finish it completely.
  • Nudging four colours closer at once usually leaves no room to manoeuvre.
  • The board starts full, so every move rearranges rather than adds.
  • Works in portrait or landscape, so it fits whatever screen you are on.
