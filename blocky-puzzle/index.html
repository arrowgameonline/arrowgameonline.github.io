About Blocky Puzzle

Blocky Puzzle is a block-placement game in the Block Blast mould. Drag the available coloured pieces onto the grid and fill a complete row or column to clear it.

Adventure mode chases a score target, Classic runs forever, and a limited swap tool bails you out of an impossible piece set. Without a falling timer, every bad placement is entirely your own. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Choose Adventure for goal-based stages or Classic for an endless score run.

  • Compare all the pieces at the bottom against the empty regions on the grid.
  • Place the most awkward piece first, while you still have room to absorb it.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear it and open fresh space.

Quick Tips

  • Look at every piece in the tray before you commit to the first one.
  • The swap tool has only two uses, so it is an emergency option.
  • Clearing a line is the only way to recover space.
  • Keep the board tidy under slowly worsening odds.
