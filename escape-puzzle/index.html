About Escape Puzzle

Escape Puzzle is a match-3 game with a rescue objective instead of a score target. You swap adjacent gems to line up three or more of a colour.

Each level asks you to remove something specific from the king's route — boulders, screws, a locked chamber, a closing hazard. A huge match in the wrong column is worth less than a small one in the right place. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Identify what the level actually wants: rocks cleared, a chamber opened, a hazard survived.

  • Tap two neighbouring gems to swap them and form a line of three or more.
  • Work the column that sits above your objective so the board settles usefully.
  • Hold power-ups until the objective genuinely needs them instead of firing them early.

Quick Tips

  • Read the objective before your first swap.
  • Cleared screws stay cleared, so early matches permanently reshape the route.
  • Gravity matters — clearing the correct column decides where stones settle.
  • Larger chains unlock row, column and hazard power-ups worth saving.
