About Arrow Tap Away

Arrow Tap Away is arrow logic on a hexagonal board with a move counter watching you. Tap a colored tile and it tries to travel along its printed arrow.

If the lane is open it leaves. If anything is in the way it bounces straight back — and you still lose the move. Guessing is possible here; it is just expensive. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Tap a colored hex to send it along the arrow printed on it.

  • Before tapping, trace the full lane — open space and interior holes both count as valid exits.
  • If a tile bounces back, find the tile sitting in its lane and clear that one first.
  • Watch MOVES at all times and never repeat a tap you already know is blocked.

Quick Tips

  • Work from exposed arrows that already face open space inward.
  • The hex grid means six travel directions instead of four.
  • Hammer unlocks at Level 6 and destroys one tile you pick.
  • Save TNT and Drill for the boards that genuinely deserve them.
