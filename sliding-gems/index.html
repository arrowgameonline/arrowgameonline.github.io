About Sliding Gems

Sliding Gems is a block puzzle where the pieces slide rather than drop. Coloured gem bars of different lengths sit on the grid and you move them to complete lines.

A bar's length is fixed, so the question is never whether it fits but whether it can get where you need it. Coin-priced power-ups, including a bomb, can break a jammed board. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Read the grid and note which bars have clear lanes to move along.

  • Slide a bar toward a line you can actually complete.
  • Check that a bar has room to travel before you count on it.
  • Complete rows or columns to clear them and free up lanes.

Quick Tips

  • Think about travel room, not just the gap you are trying to fill.
  • Hold power-ups for a board that is genuinely jammed rather than merely awkward.
  • A visible score, level and best-score target drive the run.
  • Works in portrait or landscape on phone and desktop.
