About Silly Sky

Silly Sky is an air-traffic logic game above an island airport. Select an incoming plane, draw the route it should fly, and land it on the runway matching its colour.

Several aircraft share the airspace at once, so every new route has to account for the ones already moving. A collision reduces the landings you had already banked. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard from the Island screen before you start.

  • Tap an incoming aircraft to begin planning its route.
  • Draw a path that brings it onto the runway matching its colour.
  • Keep enough distance from routes already in motion, and revise a path rather than letting two aircraft converge.

Quick Tips

  • A safe landing starts long before the runway.
  • Never draw a path through airspace another plane is about to occupy.
  • Spacing beats speed every time.
  • Hard mode is waiting if you can hold three moving things in your head at once.
