What Is Arrow Puzzle?

Arrow Puzzle is the cleanest version of the arrow-game idea. Every colored line has a head. That head is the only way it can travel. If another line sits in the lane, the tap does nothing and you try a different piece.

Puzzle Game Tips

These boards reward a longer look more than a faster tap.

  • Start at the edge. Exposed arrows and leftover gaps are the free moves.
  • Place the awkward piece first. In block games, the leftover space has to stay usable.
  • Finish one colour. In sorting games, half-done groups leave no room.
  • Watch Remaining. In Arrow Fall, the lowest open arrow keeps the run alive.
  • Use fullscreen. Dense hexes and 3D cubes are easier to read when they are larger.
  • Change one habit. Use a failed board to test a clearer first move.
