About Traffic Escape

Traffic Escape is a top-down junction puzzle where every car wears its route on the roof. Tap a car and it drives the marked path — straight, left, right, or a U-turn.

Your job is finding an order that gets everyone out without two cars meeting in the middle. Hitting a pedestrian fails the level outright. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Check every roof arrow first and note which cars go straight, turn, or swing around.

  • Trace each route through the junction and look for paths that cross another car or a crossing.
  • Release one car whose entire route is clear, then let it finish before tapping the next.
  • Never send a car through an occupied pedestrian lane.

Quick Tips

  • A right-turning car on the outside edge is often the safest first move.
  • Do not release a second car while the first is still moving.
  • Colliding cars return to their starting spots, so a mistake costs progress, not the level.
  • Save the helicopter for a car that truly blocks everything.
