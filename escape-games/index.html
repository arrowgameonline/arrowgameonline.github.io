What Is Traffic Escape?

Traffic Escape turns a static unblock puzzle into moving traffic. Cars travel over time, so two safe-looking releases can still collide mid-junction. Pedestrians are the real danger: hitting one fails the level outright.

Key Traffic Escape and escape game features:

  • Routes printed on each car, including turns and U-turns.
  • Timing as well as direction — wait until a car finishes.
  • Pedestrian crossings that fail the level if you ignore them.

How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

The player at the top of this page opens a free arrow game online directly in your browser. Wait for the loading screen, read every roof arrow, and use fullscreen when the junction gets busy.

Read the Whole Junction

Check every roof before you tap. Note which cars go straight, turn, or swing around, and which paths cross a pedestrian lane.

Release One Car at a Time

Send a car whose entire route is clear, then let it finish. The common mistake is releasing a second car while the first is still moving.

Protect the Crossings

A collision sends cars back to their starting spots. Hitting a pedestrian ends the level. Patience is free.

Escape Game Tips

These games punish the obvious first tap more than they punish a slow start.

  • Trace the exit backward. The piece you want last tells you what has to move first.
  • Wait for moving traffic. A clear lane can still be occupied a second later.
  • Keep one bay free. In Bus Parking Out, a full terminal with the wrong colours is a soft lock.
  • Read the objective. In Escape Puzzle, the right column matters more than a huge match.
  • Use fullscreen. Junctions and rooms are easier to search when they fill the screen.
  • Note every odd detail. In the dollhouse, one room explains another.
