About 3D Arrow Eliminate

3D Arrow Eliminate wraps arrow logic around a cube you can spin with your finger. Every block carries an arrow and can only leave if nothing blocks the route behind it.

Half the structure is facing away from you, so turning the shape is part of solving it. The game guarantees at least one block can always leave. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Drag to rotate the structure and check the front, both sides, the top, and the depth behind each arrow.

  • Find a block whose arrow points out of the shape with nothing in the way.
  • Tap it — a valid block flies off and exposes new edges.
  • Re-inspect the new outline before tapping again instead of staying on one face.

Quick Tips

  • After every removal, turn the cube before you tap again.
  • Depth matters as much as direction — a block can be walled in from behind.
  • Level Challenge gives you a tap allowance; a blocked tap still spends one.
  • Use reverse, auto-clear, or reseat tools when a cube looks locked.
