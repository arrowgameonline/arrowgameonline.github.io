About Arrow Fall

Arrow Fall takes the standard arrow rule and drops the floor out from under it. Colored lines sit above a red limit and the whole board sinks as you play.

You still only clear arrows whose lane is open — you just have to do it before the stack lands on the line. Remaining starts at 100% and drops as arrows leave. How to Play This Free Arrow Game Online

This free arrow game online is simple to start. Read an arrowhead and trace its lane toward the edge of the board.

  • Tap only when no other colored line sits in that lane.
  • Watch Remaining fall with each successful clear — 0% is the finish line.
  • Prioritise the lowest arrows. Clearing near the red line buys you time.

Quick Tips

  • Height is the resource you are spending, and it only ever runs one way.
  • Play the lowest open arrow first, even when a bigger clear sits higher up.
  • A blocked tap does not reset anything, but the board keeps falling.
  • Three shields in the top bar are your margin for error.
